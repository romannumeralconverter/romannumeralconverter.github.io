Roman Numerals 1 to 100: Complete Chart and Easy Guide

Use this guide to find any Roman numeral from 1 through 100, understand the symbols behind it, and recognize common subtractive combinations.

Updated October 30, 2026 · 6 minute read

Roman numerals use letters instead of the place-value digits used by the modern decimal system. The most important symbols are I, V, X, L, C, D, and M. Once these values are familiar, the numbers from 1 to 100 become straightforward to read and write.

How to read the chart

Most Roman numerals are formed by adding symbols from left to right. For example, VIII is 5 + 1 + 1 + 1, which equals 8. Symbols are normally arranged from larger values to smaller values.

Subtractive notation changes that rule when a smaller symbol appears before a larger one. IV means 5 − 1 = 4, while IX means 10 − 1 = 9.
