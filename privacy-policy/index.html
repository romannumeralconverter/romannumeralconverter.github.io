Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28

Information collection

roman-numeral.org is a static website. Converter inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to a backend database by this site.

Local storage

This site stores a single setting in your browser localStorage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode).

Personal data

We do not require accounts and do not intentionally collect personal data through the converter itself.

Contact

If you use the Contact page, your message is sent through your email client using mailto. Data handling for that message depends on your email provider.

Third-party services

This static implementation does not require external scripts for conversion functionality.

Policy updates

This page may be updated as site functionality changes.
