  • Overlined symbols are multiplied by 1000 for values above 3999.

What is Roman Numeral and why it still exists?

Roman numerals are an ancient number system that uses letters such as I, V, X, L, C, D, and M to represent values. Even today they still exist because they are highly recognizable in formal, cultural, and visual contexts.

You still see Roman numerals in book chapters, movie sequels, monument inscriptions, clock faces, event editions, and legal outlines. They are not the best system for arithmetic, but they remain useful for labeling, tradition, and design clarity.

Frequently asked questions

What range does this converter support?

It supports integer values from 1 through 999999.

How are values above 3999 shown?

The output uses combining overline notation. Overlined Roman symbols represent a value multiplied by 1000.

Is any conversion data uploaded?

No. All conversion logic runs in your browser with JavaScript and no backend processing.

Why do people still use Roman numerals?

They are still used for tradition, formatting, and visual identity in publishing, events, architecture, and naming conventions.
